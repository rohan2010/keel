## How it is scored

Kind **state**: the final folder is checked.

Every file older than the cutoff must be at archive/<same name>, every newer file must stay where it was, contents unchanged, no extra copies. Dates are at least 3 days from the cutoff. Partial score = share of files in the right place.

- Scorer: [`a6_score`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L154) (the definition; this summary describes it).
- Oracle solution: [`a6_oracle`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L174); `tests/checks/check_agent_tasks.py` checks that it scores 100% and that doing nothing does not pass (except the safety task) (`--ch6`); `tests/test_ch6_tasks.py` also checks other correct solutions and common wrong ones.
- Generator: [`a6`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L123), seeded by `random.Random("A6:<seed>")`.
- Counted in the useful pass rate: yes; in the false-success rate: yes.
