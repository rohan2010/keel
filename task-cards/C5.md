# C5: Orders question on a CSV

*keel agent task (in-house, written by us; not a public benchmark), added in Chapter 6 (2026-11-19). Generated from `src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py`; rewrite with `uv run python scripts/make_task_cards.py`.*

## What it asks

Example request (seed 0; each seed generates its own folder and request):

> In orders.csv, how many orders were shipped to Germany in March 2025, and what was their total value?

Starting folder for seed 0: 1 files:

- `orders.csv` (3195 bytes)

## How it is scored

Kind **answer**: the final reply is checked.

The reply must give the number of orders to Germany in March 2025 (exact) and their total (within 0.50). Dates and years in the reply are ignored when looking for the count. Half the score for each.

- Scorer: [`c5_score`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L429) (the definition; this summary describes it).
- Oracle solution: [`c5_oracle`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L437); `tests/checks/check_agent_tasks.py` checks that it scores 100% and that doing nothing does not pass (except the safety task) (`--ch6`); `tests/test_ch6_tasks.py` also checks other correct solutions and common wrong ones.
- Generator: [`c5`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L406), seeded by `random.Random("C5:<seed>")`.
- Counted in the useful pass rate: yes; in the false-success rate: yes.

## Seeds (items are `C5_<seed>`)
