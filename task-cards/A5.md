## How it is scored

Kind **state**: the final folder is checked.

Every generated file must exist exactly once (matched by content hash), directly inside export/, and export/ must have no subfolders left. Same-name files may be renamed any way. Partial score = share of files in place. Labels: lost files, files not in export/, extra copies left, subfolders left.

- Scorer: [`a5_score`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L78) (the definition; this summary describes it).
- Oracle solution: [`a5_oracle`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L101); `tests/checks/check_agent_tasks.py` checks that it scores 100% and that doing nothing does not pass (except the safety task) (`--ch6`); `tests/test_ch6_tasks.py` also checks other correct solutions and common wrong ones.
- Generator: [`a5`](../src/keel/evals/tasks_ch6.py#L52), seeded by `random.Random("A5:<seed>")`.
- Counted in the useful pass rate: yes; in the false-success rate: yes.
